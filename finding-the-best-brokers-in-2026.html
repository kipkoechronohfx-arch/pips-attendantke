Why Execution Speed Matters

In forex trading, especially during high-impact news, prices can move rapidly. A broker with fast execution ensures your orders are filled at the desired price, minimizing "slippage". Slippage occurs when there's a difference between the expected price of a trade and the price at which the trade is actually executed.

The Importance of Tight Spreads

The "spread" is the difference between the buy (ask) and sell (bid) price of a currency pair. It's essentially the cost of the trade. Tighter spreads mean lower trading costs, which is crucial for profitability, especially for day traders and scalpers.
